Guardianship For International Students

Guardianship services provide care, guidance, and support for international students studying away from home. Guardians help students stay safe, adjust to a new environment, and receive assistance throughout their academic journey.

If you’re under 18, we’ll find you a guardian

In Australia, all international students who are under 18 years of age need to have approved welfare arrangements in place before they arrive in the country. DN Australia has teamed up with ISA Student Advocates to assist in providing welfare services to our students. Your safety and well-being are our top priorities throughout your study journey.


Guardians you can trust

DREAM NEST has access to a nationwide network of caring and experienced guardians. If you don’t have a family member to look after you in your destination city, talk to us about arranging a guardian for you until you turn 18.


Reliable care and support

Your guardian’s goal is to support you in your study abroad journey. You’ll also find them helpful in other ways like settling you in and giving study advice. They’ll even help you meet new people and discover social and extracurricular activities.
