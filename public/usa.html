Understanding each step of the admission process ensures a smooth transition to studying in the USA. Take notes! Application Requirements

• Statement of Purpose (SOP)

USA Student Visa Process


To apply for a US student visa, you must first apply and be accepted by an institution in the USA that is certified by the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP).


USA study visa requirements may vary since every visa type has certain eligibility criteria to meet.


Once accepted, you will receive a Form I-20 from the institution's international student office, which is a paper record of your information in the database called the Student and Exchange Visitor Information System (SEVIS).

Scholarships


The concept of free education is not actually available for international students in the USA. And this is where scholarships come in!


Several scholarships are available for Pakistani students at various US universities. To apply for these scholarships, you need to submit the required documents, including academic transcripts, SOP and a letter of recommendation. These scholarships are open for a limited time and students are often advised to gather their documents in advance to avoid any further inconvenience.
