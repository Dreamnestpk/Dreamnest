Why Study in Canada?


Studying in Canada can be a great learning experience for a Pakistani student. Many students choose Canada as their study destination because its universities are known around the world for their high-quality education and innovative research. This opens doors to numerous job opportunities for them across the globe. Additionally, Canada is a multicultural country, it's easier for students from different backgrounds to blend in with others, making them feel at home even when they're far away.


Another advantage for students and their families is that tuition fees in Canada are more affordable than those in other top study destinations. Many colleges and universities also offer scholarships to help cover costs, making higher education more accessible for international students.


The Post-Graduation Work Permit and other programs in Canada facilitate finding work while you're studying and after you graduate. The Post-Graduation Work Permit gives students a chance to get valuable work experience and apply for permanent residency if they wish to stay in Canada as permanent residents.

Cost of Studying in Canada


The cost of living for international students in Canada varies a lot depending on their location and lifestyle. Rent, food, transportation and personal expenses in Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal and other big cities tend to be higher, averaging up to CAD 3,500 a month. Living in smaller cities and towns is relatively affordable, with monthly costs of about CAD 1,200.

Student Visa Requirements


Pakistani students who want to study in Canada should hold a valid passport along with other travel documents. They must also get a valid study permit from the Canadian Department of Home Affairs. In addition, students must have an acceptance letter from a recognised Canadian and prove that they have enough money to pay for their tuition, living expenses and transportation expenses. If necessary, applicants should complete biometrics and health checks, maintain a clean criminal record, and be in good health.


The standard fee for a visa application is CAD 150, and if biometrics are required, there is an extra fee of CAD 85. It is best to apply early so that processing times can be taken into account and to make sure you get into the proper academic intake. To make sure that students can start their studies in Canada on time, it is important to meet all these requirements.


Student Visa Application Process


The process for applying for a Canadian student visa from Pakistan involves the following steps:


• Secure Admission:


Apply to and get accepted to a Designated Learning Institution (DLI) in Canada.


• Get the Necessary Papers:


Get all the papers you need, such as the acceptance letter, passport, bank statements, academic transcripts and English language test scores (like IELTS or TOEFL).


• Create an Account Online:


Set up an account on the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) website.


• Submit the Application:


Complete the student visa application form online and submit it. Upload all the necessary papers and pay the processing fees.


• Provide Biometric Data:


Visit a specific collection point to submit your biometric data. If the visa officer asks you to, you must be ready to go to an interview.


• Wait for Approval:


Once your application is approved, you will receive a Port of Entry (POE) Letter of Introduction.


Intakes in Canada


Canadian universities have two main intakes every year, which gives international students a lot of flexibility and choice when it comes to planning their studies. The Fall intake, which starts in September, is the most popular and has the most programs available at different schools.


The May summer intake has fewer program options, but it works well for some specialised courses or part-time studies.


You must plan carefully to meet deadlines and get into the intake that best fits your needs. The right intake depends on factors such as how well prepared you are to begin your educational journey and the time required for visa processing.


Scholarships & Financial Aid


Financial support is available through scholarships and grants to help Pakistani students manage the cost of studying in Canada. These are available at both government and university levels:


• Government Scholarships:


Offered by the Canadian government and international bodies to support talented students.


• University Scholarships:


Many Canadian universities provide merit-based scholarships, entrance awards, and financial aid packages. Examples include the University of Calgary International Entrance Award and the Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship.


Students should review and apply for scholarships early to take full advantage of these opportunities.


Job Prospects in Canada


You can work full-time during breaks while studying in Canada, but you can only work 20 hours per week while studying. Many programs offer co-op opportunities that provide valuable real-world experience.


As a graduate international student, you will have the opportunity to apply for the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP). This enables you to work in Canada for up to three years. This experience increases your chances of securing a job and provides pathways to obtain permanent residency.
