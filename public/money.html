💳 Money Transfer

Our Money Transfer service helps international students securely send tuition fees, visa payments, and living expenses directly to verified universities and institutions worldwide. We ensure fast processing, low charges, and complete transparency for your study abroad journey.

📌 How to Transfer Money to University

1. Get official offer letter from university.
2. Confirm tuition fee and bank details.
3. Fill the transfer form carefully.
4. Send payment via secure method.
5. Receive confirmation from university.
6. Save receipt for visa and records.
